# l1nk.ai

A LinkedIn coach you text. It reads your account and tells you what to post.

Write-up, not source code.

## What it is

Every LinkedIn tool wants you to open a dashboard. I don't open dashboards. I open iMessage.

l1nk sits behind a phone number. You text it, and it talks to you like someone who has actually read your account rather than a generic content generator.

- You text it, it texts back. No app, no login, no tab.
- Text it "post idea: ..." and it saves it. Text "show my ideas" and they come back. The point is catching an idea in the eight seconds you have it.
- It reads your profile, your recent posts and your comments, so the advice is based on what you actually posted.
- It drafts posts, DMs and replies, sends them to you, and does nothing until you say yes or thumbs up it.

## The one rule

It never writes in my voice.

Not "tries not to". It can't. The part that posts only accepts text I gave it. That's a hard constraint in the code, because a prompt is a suggestion and this isn't.

So it's dictate and send, not ghostwrite and approve. It's good at what to post about, when, and who to reply to. My voice is the only thing I have on that platform, so it doesn't get to touch it.

Two things follow from that:

- Nothing happens without a yes.
- There's a hard cap on how much it can do per day, set in the code. Not a setting, not a prompt. A number it can't argue its way past.

## What I learned

**The approval step is the product.** I thought the yes/no would get annoying. It's the reason I trust it enough to leave it running.

**A thumbs up is a great confirm button.** It's the least effort way to say yes on a phone, and it's the difference between using this daily and dropping it.

**Put agents where people already are.** The same thing as a web app would have died in week one.

**Fake the integrations first.** The whole conversation was built and tuned against fake data before anything real was connected, so the interesting problems got solved while the boring ones were still fake.

## Status

Working prototype, running on my own account.
